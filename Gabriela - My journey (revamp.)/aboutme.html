My biography



Hello! My name is Gabriela.
I was 17 when I started working on this blog,
but due to other responsibilities, it ended up collecting dust.
However, I'd really love to get back to
working on it and to have it see the light of day.
Unfortunately, I lost some of
the media that I was eager to share,
but at least I've got some room for the new ones
I'm just as psyched to showcase!
I've done photography ever since the beginning of my adolescent years and
I managed to put my skills into pretty great use - for
both volunteering and just a few other projects, even if, most of
the time, it's just me and my phone against the world.
I would like to issue a sincere thank you to everyone
who has inspired me throughout the years to continue
doing this, even if it's nothing short of just a hobby. 💜
